Listing tmux sessions and opening them in Terminal are now separate switches. `RAFIQ_TMUX=1` lists your tmux sessions without Terminal, and `RAFIQ_TERMINAL=1` lists and opens them. A plain shell, a session with no agent in it, no longer opens in Terminal with `RAFIQ_TERMINAL=1` alone: name it in `RAFIQ_TERMINAL_SHELLS`. A session that cannot open shows no Terminal toggle.
